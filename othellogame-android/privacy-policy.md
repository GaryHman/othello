# Privacy Policy — Reversi

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Reversi ("the app") is an offline board game. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use it.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files access.
- The app does not send any information over the internet.

## Data stored on your device
The app saves your game settings (for example "show hints" and "colorblind mode") locally on your device so they are
remembered next time. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Children
The app contains no ads, chat, purchases inside the app, or data collection, and is suitable for all ages.

## Changes
If a future version adds online features (such as online multiplayer), this policy will be updated before that version
is released, and the date above will change.
